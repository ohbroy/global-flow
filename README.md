# Global Flow

Rebuild the website as a REAL, premium global logistics and freight company website, NOT a fictional concept and NOT a 3D/Three.js demonstration.

The main goal is a cinematic scroll-driven storytelling experience inspired by the smooth storytelling and transitions of high-end automotive/aviation websites such as jeskojets.com.

MOST IMPORTANT CONCEPT

The website should visually tell the story of a shipment moving around the world.

As the visitor scrolls, they should feel like they are physically travelling with the cargo:

AIR → SKY → SEA → SHIP → PORT → LAND → DELIVERY → GLOBAL NETWORK

The scenes must transition into one another naturally.

Do NOT make every section look like a separate webpage.

Do NOT use hard horizontal divider lines between scenes.

Do NOT create a generic SaaS layout.

Do NOT make the main experience dependent on 3D models.

Use high-quality realistic photography and video footage, combined with CSS/GSAP animation, parallax, masking, scaling, cropping and carefully timed transitions.

The result should look like a professional real-world logistics company website.

OPENING — AIRCRAFT

The aircraft continues moving.

* position

TRANSITION — SKY TO SEA

The camera travels downward through the atmosphere.

The horizon becomes visible.

The ocean appears.

The transition should feel like the visitor is travelling from the sky toward the surface of the Earth.

Do NOT suddenly replace the aircraft scene with a completely unrelated section.

The visual elements should overlap during the transition.
